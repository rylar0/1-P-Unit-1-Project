Genshin Impact Meta

Genshin Impact is an ongoing rpg with varying characters that provide different mechanics to the game meant to give the player a more enjoyable experience.

What is meta?

Meta refers to the characters in the game that are the most powerful, they do the most damage or are the best supports. As the game progresses, however, the meta changes a lot which makes it difficult for some players to keep up.
